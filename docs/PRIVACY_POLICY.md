# Privacy Policy (draft for FYP prototype)

Last updated: [date]

## What we collect
- **Account data:** email, hashed password.
- **Message text:** only the text you submit or explicitly trigger the extension to read.
- **Usage data:** feature use and feedback ratings (no message content in analytics).

## What we do not do
- We do not read pages in the background. The extension reads text only after you press the shortcut or button.
- We do not read password or payment fields.
- We do not send messages on your behalf.
- We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

## How message text is used
It is sent to our server to generate suggestions. Likely personal identifiers (emails, phone numbers, card numbers) are redacted in your browser first. Your messages are used to train models only if you explicitly opt in.

## Storage
History is **off by default**. If you turn it on, saved items are encrypted and kept for the retention period in `DATA_RETENTION.md`.

## Your rights
View, export and delete your data at any time from Settings. Account deletion removes all stored data.

## Security
See `SECURITY.md`. We use encryption in transit and at rest, access controls and rate limiting.

## Third parties
Hosting providers and an email provider for verification messages. No advertising or analytics trackers that read message content.

## Children
The service is not intended for users under 16.
