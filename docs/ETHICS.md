# Ethics and Responsible AI

## 1. Principles
Transparency, user control, privacy, fairness, honesty, human-in-the-loop.

## 2. Human-in-the-loop
SaylientAI only **suggests**. The user reviews, edits and sends. The system never sends messages automatically.

## 3. Honesty and manipulation
- The assistant must not generate deceptive, threatening, harassing or coercive messages.
- It must not invent facts (amounts, dates, agreements) not supplied by the user.
- Firm tone is allowed; hostility and intimidation are not.

## 4. Bias and fairness
- Politeness norms vary by culture; the dataset documents its cultural assumptions (primarily English professional norms).
- Review samples for gender, nationality and regional bias; test with diverse names and contexts.
- Offer tone options instead of one "correct" style.

## 5. Privacy
Minimal data, explicit triggers, redaction, opt-in history and training. See `PRIVACY_POLICY.md`.

## 6. Dependence and transparency
Explanations teach the user why a reply works so they build their own skill. Label outputs as AI-generated suggestions.

## 7. Misuse and limits
- Not legal or financial advice; no guarantees about outcomes with clients.
- Block harassment, scams, phishing and impersonation requests.
- Provide a report/feedback channel.

## 8. Research ethics
- Informed consent for user studies, anonymized data, right to withdraw.
- Supervisor / department approval before collecting participant data.
- No real client conversations in the dataset without consent and anonymization.

## 9. Accountability
Model and dataset versions are tracked; issues are logged and fixed with regression tests.
